# Love is Foundation
# Map of Co-Creators Development Framework

## Purpose

The goal of redesigning the Map of Co-Creators is to turn it into the main hub for discovering everything happening within Love Is Foundation. Instead of only showing where people are located, it will become a place where users can discover events, connect with people, explore communities, and find organizations around the world. The map will work alongside the Love Is Foundation website and engagement platform to help people find opportunities that are relevant to them.

---

# Main Features

## Interactive Map

The map will remain the centre of the platform. Instead of only displaying people, it will display different types of content depending on what the user is looking for. This includes people, events, communities, and organizations.

Every marker on the map will be clickable and open more information about that person, event, community, or organization. The map will update automatically whenever filters are changed so users can quickly find what they're looking for.

---

## Master Calendar

The platform will contain one master calendar where all Love Is Foundation events are displayed. Instead of searching through different spaces or websites, users can come to one place and see everything that is happening.

Users will be able to browse upcoming events, filter events by different categories, and open an event to view more information. The calendar and the map will work together, meaning events shown on the map will also appear in the calendar.

---

## Member Connection Hub

The Map will also act as a place for members to discover and connect with one another.

Each member will have their own profile that may include:

- Name
- Profile Picture
- Bio
- Country / City
- Interests
- Seven Aspects
- Organizations they belong to

The goal is to help people discover others with similar interests or who are located nearby while still allowing users to control what information they want to share.

---

## Organization Access

Organizations will be able to join the Map of Co-Creators and become part of the Love Is Foundation ecosystem.

Each organization will have its own area within the platform containing information such as:

- Organization name
- Description
- Members
- Communities
- Events

Organizations will also be able to create their own events.

Some events may be public for everyone to discover, while others may only be available to members or employees of that organization.

For example, if someone belongs to Ashoka, once they sign into their account they would automatically gain access to Ashoka-only events. Someone who is not part of Ashoka would only see public events and would not be able to access private organization events.

This allows organizations to use the same platform while still keeping their own private communities and events.

---

# Navigation

The main navigation of the Map will be based around what users are trying to discover.

Main Categories:

- Events
- People
- Communities
- Organizations

Selecting one of these categories will update the map and the information shown on the page.

# Filter System

The filtering system is one of the most important parts of the platform. Instead of scrolling through hundreds of events or people, users can narrow everything down to exactly what they are interested in.

## Seven Aspects

- Source & Resources (Flow)
- Divine Human Potential
- Presence & Being (Me)
- Engagement & Communion (We)
- Nature & Nurture
- Community & Inclusion (Us)
- Service & Offerings (Life)

These aspects allow users to discover content that aligns with the area of growth or experience they are looking for.

## Interests

Users can also filter by areas of interest.

- Infrastructure
- Justice
- Media
- Relations
- Science
- Spirituality
- Arts
- Economics
- Education
- Environment
- Governance
- Health

These interests apply across events, people, communities, and organizations whenever relevant.

## Event Format

Users can choose how they want to participate.

- In Person
- Online
- Hybrid

## Event Type

Different kinds of events can be filtered separately.

- Workshop
- Discussion
- Meetup
- Networking
- Walk / Hike

## Date

Users can quickly filter events by time.

- Today
- Tomorrow
- This Week
- This Weekend
- Next Week
- This Month
- Custom

## Time

- Morning
- Afternoon
- Evening
- Night

## Location

Users will be able to discover events based on how close they are to their current location.

For Example: 
- Within 5 km
- Within 10 km
- Within 25 km
- Within 50 km
- Within 100 km
- Anywhere

The map will automatically display events that fall within the selected distance.

For online events, the location filter will not be needed, since anyone can attend regardless of where they are.

## Session Duration

Users can filter events based on how long each session lasts.

- Up to 1 Hour
- 2 Hours
- 3 Hours
- Half Day
- Full Day

---

## Event Commitment

Users can filter events based on the overall commitment required.

- One-Time Event
- Multiple Sessions
- Ongoing

## People Filters

When searching for people, users can also filter by:

- Friends
- Interests
- Organization
- Location

This makes it easier to discover people with similar interests or people who are nearby.

---

# Data & User Management

The system will be responsible for storing and managing everything that appears on the platform.

This includes:

- User Accounts
- Member Profiles
- Events
- Organizations
- Communities

It will also manage:

- User permissions
- Privacy settings
- Event information
- Search and filtering
- Organization memberships
- Event visibility

The system will determine what each user can access based on their account and the organizations they belong to.
